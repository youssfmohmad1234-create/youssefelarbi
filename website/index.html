BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

ABOUT ME

“

Hi, I’m Youssef El Araby, a Media Buyer and Performance Marketing Specialist with 6+ years of experience in digital advertising.

I started my career in Marketing in 2017 as a Chat Moderator, where I gained hands-on experience in customer communication, brand interaction, and understanding audience behavior.

In 2018, I moved into Sales, gaining valuable experience in understanding customer needs, handling objections, and turning conversations into opportunities.

In 2020, I made a career shift into Media Buying, focusing on performance-driven advertising across Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked with brands across different industries and markets, gaining experience in building, managing, testing, and optimizing digital advertising campaigns.

Alongside Media Buying, I developed strong experience in Content Creation and Creative Strategy. I’ve attended and participated in content shoots for the brands I worked with, giving me hands-on experience with the creative process behind the ads — from developing concepts and messaging to understanding what type of content can perform effectively in paid campaigns.

Today, I combine Media Buying, Performance Marketing, Content Creation, and Creative Thinking to build campaigns driven by both data and strong creative execution.
